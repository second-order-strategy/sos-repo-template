# 1. Record major decisions

**Status:** Accepted · **Recorded:** 2026-11-14 · **Classification:** Public

## Context

Major design choices need to be traceable (CS-03.3, EOS-DES-01).

## Decision

Each major choice gets a numbered record here: context, alternatives, consequences, and what would make us revisit it.

## Consequences

An auditor or a new team can see why the system looks the way it does.

## Revisit when

The record format stops fitting the work.
